Food Worth Coming Back For.

The story behind Ember & Plate.

Ember & Plate started with a simple idea: great food doesn't need to feel intimidating or ridiculously expensive.

We wanted a place in Lusaka where you can walk in after work, in your weekend clothes or with the whole family, and eat something properly good. Somewhere the fries are hot, the chicken comes off the flame with real char on it and nobody makes you feel like you picked the wrong fork.

So we cook over fire, keep the plates generous and keep the prices fair. Local favourites like tilapia and nshima or roti and beef stew sit on the same menu as burgers and pasta, because that's what people actually want to eat.

Come for a quick lunch. Stay for dessert. Bring a friend, or ten.
